Quick Help

  • Left click to use current tool.
  • Use Build to place towers and auto-connect walls.
  • In Build, right click or press Esc to unlink the selected tower.
  • Right click or middle click and drag to pan.
  • Use WASD or the arrow keys to move around the map.
  • Shift/Ctrl/Cmd + click to multi-select in Select / Move.
  • Double-click an object to select every matching type, colour, and health.
  • Draw Hole: click vertices, then click the first vertex to close. Esc cancels.
  • Hold Ctrl to temporarily disable object and grid snapping.
  • Ctrl+C copies selection; left click places the ghost copy.
  • Save a selection under Custom Shapes to reuse it between maps and sessions.
  • Drag the blue rotation handle to rotate a selected group.
  • Drag a side or corner handle to scale; drag the dotted outline to move.
  • Draw one or more mirror axes, then use live mode or mirror a selection once.
  • In Select / Move, drag a mirror axis directly to reposition it; axes are never part of group selection.
  • Mouse wheel to zoom at cursor.
